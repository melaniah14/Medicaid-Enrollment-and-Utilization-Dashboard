# Medicaid-Enrollment-and-Utilization-Dashboard
Analysis of Maryland Medicaid enrollment, renewal outcomes, procedural disenrollments, and pharmacy utilization (2023–2026) using Python, SQL, and Power BI to support healthcare management decision-making in my honors thesis

This project uses publicly available Medicaid enrollment, eligibility processing, and pharmacy utilization data from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to examine changes in Maryland Medicaid coverage between 2023 and 2026. Using Python, SQL, and Power BI, I analyzed Medicaid and CHIP enrollment trends, renewal outcomes, procedural disenrollments, prescription utilization, and pharmacy reimbursements.

Maryland Medicaid enrollment declined by approximately 24.14% between May 2023 and May 2026. However, prescription volume declined at a slower rate than enrollment. I used Pearson correlation analysis to examine the relationship between quarterly enrollment changes and prescription utilization, finding a very weak positive correlation (r = 0.125) between enrollment changes and prescription volume changes.

The project also examines procedural disenrollments, which accounted for more than 80% of renewal disenrollments in most complete quarters analyzed. These findings were incorporated into an interactive healthcare management case study focused on preparing for upcoming Medicaid policy changes in 2027 and using data to support outreach, resource allocation, and financial planning decisions.
